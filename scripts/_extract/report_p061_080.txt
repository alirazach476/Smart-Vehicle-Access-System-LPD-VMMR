5.2.4 Post Processing:          
The reconstructed license plate text is generated through a deterministic post -processing stage 
after the successful detection of individual characters within the cropped license plate region. 
During this process, all detected characters are first organize d according to the horizontal 
coordinates of their center positions, ensuring a left -to-right reading order regardless of the 
sequence in which they were detected by the recognition model. After sorting, the detected 
characters are separated into two groups consisting of alphabetic letters and numeric digits. These 
groups are then combined following the standard Pakistani license plate format, which typically 
contains up to three letters followed by a hyphen and up to four digits, such as LEB-1481 or RND-
5050. This spatial sorting approach is more robust than a fixed sequential reading method because 
it effectively handles variations in character spacing, slight detection order inconsistencies, and 
minor alignment errors commonly observed on real -world licen se plates. As a result, the 
reconstructed plate number is more accurate and reliable even under challenging imaging 
conditions. The finalized license plate string is subsequently forwarded to the authorization 
module, where it is matched against the regist ered vehicle records stored in the database. This 
verification process supports dual -modality authentication, enabling accurate vehicle 
identification and secure access control within the proposed intelligent vehicle recognition system.
